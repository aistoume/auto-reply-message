### Description（4000 字符）

**EN**
```
Some messages are hard to answer. Not because typing is hard — because you can't find the words that hold your ground without blowing up the relationship.

Aplomb reads the screenshot of your conversation and writes that sentence for you.

WHAT YOU GET BACK

Every draft comes with four things, not just a reply:

• Subtext — what they're actually doing with that message: applying pressure, testing you, shifting blame, setting up an ask
• Risk — if the screenshot is ambiguous or missing context, Aplomb says so instead of guessing
• The reply — ready to send, written in the tone you picked
• A heads-up — what sending this will likely set in motion, and what you still need to decide yourself

HOW IT WORKS

1. Screenshot the chat
2. Open Aplomb, pick who you're talking to, pick a tone
3. Read the draft, copy it, paste it in the chat
4. You read it. You hit send.

Aplomb never sends anything for you. It doesn't read your notifications, doesn't run in the background, doesn't take over a conversation. Every word gets past your eyes first — because the person on the other end thinks they're talking to you.

TEN TONES, ALL EDITABLE

Warm · Grateful · Yes, gladly · Light-hearted · Comforting · Decent · Asking · Pointed · Hard line · Cool it

Change the emoji, the name, the color, and — the part that matters — the instructions Aplomb follows when writing in that tone. Add your own. The presets are a starting point, not a cage.

RELATIONSHIPS

"Hard line" to your boss and "hard line" to your partner are not the same message. Pick the relationship first — family, friend, partner, client, boss, or one you define — and the same tone comes out differently.

TWO LANGUAGES AT ONCE

The reply follows THEIR language. They wrote in Japanese, you get Japanese. Mixed Chinese and English, you get the same mix back.

The analysis follows YOURS. Subtext, risk and notes are always written in the language you read, with a plain-language gloss of what the reply says. You understand every word you're about to send, even when you don't speak it well.

WHERE THE LINE IS

Baked into every tone, including the sharpest one:

• No slurs, no personal attacks, no threats, no fabrications
• The hardest tone still argues facts and consequences, nothing else
• Never commits your money, your time, or your legal exposure on your behalf — anything that's yours to decide goes in the notes, not the reply
• Conversations involving abuse, self-harm or physical safety get a conservative reply and a pointer to real help

BATTERY

New installs get a free battery. One draft costs one bar. When it runs out you can subscribe for a monthly refill — or add your own Anthropic API key in Settings and use the app with your own account instead. Both work; nothing is locked behind the subscription.

PRIVACY

Screenshots are sent to our server, forwarded to the model, and the reply comes back. They are not stored, not used for training, and not tied to a name or an account — the app never asks you to sign up.
```

**简中**
```
有些消息是真难回。难的不是打字，是想不出怎么说才既守得住底线，又不至于把关系撕破。

嘴替读你的聊天截图，替你把那句话写出来。

拿到的不只是一句回复

• 潜台词 —— 对方这句话真正在干什么：施压、试探、甩锅、还是在给要求铺垫
• 误读风险 —— 截图信息不足或有歧义时直接说明，不硬猜
• 回复正文 —— 按你选的语气写，可以直接发出去
• 发送建议 —— 这句发出去大概会引出什么，以及有哪些还得你自己拿主意

怎么用

1. 截一张聊天图
2. 打开嘴替，选对方是谁，选一个语气
3. 读一遍草稿，复制，粘回聊天框
4. 你自己检查，你自己按发送

嘴替不会替你发送，不读你的通知，不在后台跑，不接管对话。AI 写的每个字，发出去之前都经过你的眼睛——因为对面那个人以为在跟你说话。

十种语气，全部可改

温和 · 道谢 · 爽快答应 · 轻松幽默 · 安慰 · 体面 · 请求 · 绵里藏针 · 掀桌 · 冷处理

emoji、名称、颜色都能改，最关键的是——**写给 AI 的说话方式**也能改。也可以自己加。预设只是起点，不是笼子。

先选关系

对老板「掀桌」和对恋人「掀桌」，根本不是同一句话。先选关系——家人、朋友、恋人、客户、老板，或者你自己定义一个——同一个语气会写出不同的东西。

两种语言同时跑

回复正文跟着**对方**的语言。对方发日文，你拿到日文；中英夹杂，你拿到同样的夹杂。

分析跟着**你的**语言。潜台词、风险、建议永远用你读的语言写，还附一句大白话说明这条回复到底说了什么。哪怕这门语言你并不熟，你也清楚自己正要发出去的是什么。

分寸底线

写进每一个语气，包括最狠的那个：

• 不脏字、不人身攻击、不威胁、不造谣
• 最硬的语气也只讲事实和后果
• 不替你承诺钱、时间、法律责任——需要你定的写进建议，不写进正文
• 涉及家暴、自伤、人身安全的对话，正文保守，并提示走现实求助渠道

电池

新安装送一块免费电池，一次拟稿耗一格。用完可以订阅按月续电，也可以在设置里填自己的 Anthropic API key 用自己的账号跑。两条路都通，没有功能被锁在订阅后面。

隐私

截图发到我们的服务器，转给模型，回复传回来。不存档、不用于训练、不关联姓名或账号——这个 app 从来不要求你注册。
```

```
HOW TO TEST — no account needed.

1. In the Simulator/device, take a screenshot of any conversation (Messages, WhatsApp, or even a screenshot of an email — any image with text in it works).
2. Launch Aplomb. The Draft tab loads the newest screenshot from Photos.
3. Pick a relationship (Family / Friend / Partner / Client / Boss) and then a tone.
4. A draft appears within ~10 seconds: subtext, risk, the reply, and notes.

A free battery (20 drafts) is granted automatically on first launch — no sign-up, no key, nothing to enter. If it is exhausted during review, please contact us and we will top it up immediately.

WHAT THE APP DOES NOT DO
Aplomb does not send messages. It has no accessibility service, no notification access, and no background execution. The user copies the draft and pastes it themselves. The bundled keyboard extension only inserts text the user explicitly taps; it has no network access.

SCREENSHOT HANDLING
Screenshots are selected by the user from their own Photo Library, sent over HTTPS to our server (aicon.solutions), forwarded to the Anthropic API for analysis, and discarded. Nothing is retained and nothing is tied to an identity.

SUBSCRIPTIONS
Three auto-renewable subscriptions (Light / Regular / Heavy) refill the monthly battery. They are optional: users can instead enter their own Anthropic API key in Settings and use every feature with their own account. This is stated on the purchase screen.
```

先建一个订阅组，名字 `Aplomb Battery`，然后建三个商品：

| Product ID | Reference Name | 时长 | 价格 | 显示名 (EN / 中) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `aplomb.sub.lite` | Aplomb Light | 1 Month | $2.99 | Light / 轻度 |
| `aplomb.sub.plus` | Aplomb Regular | 1 Month | $6.99 | Regular / 常用 |
| `aplomb.sub.pro` | Aplomb Heavy | 1 Month | $19.99 | Heavy / 重度 |

每个商品的 **Localization** 必填（显示名 + 描述），少一项就一直卡在 "Missing Metadata"，app 里 `Product.products` 拿不到东西，订阅页会一直转圈。

描述文案：
- Light：`100 drafts a month. For the occasional message you can't just answer.` / `每月 100 次拟稿。给偶尔那几条不好回的消息。`
- Regular：`300 drafts a month. A few every day.` / `每月 300 次拟稿。每天都有几条。`
- Heavy：`1000 drafts a month. All day, every workday.` / `每月 1000 次拟稿。整个工作日都在用。`

三个商品还各要一张 **审核截图**（订阅页的截图即可）。
